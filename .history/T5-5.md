## Goal

Take and follow orders in the browser: customers, the order list, a new order with its lines, and one order's progress.

## Your decisions

| Question | Choice |
|---|---|
| The mockup shows line totals and an order total before saving; the conventions forbid money math with JavaScript numbers | An estimate in whole cents (`utils/money.ts`), labelled as an estimate; the saved amounts come from the backend. Added to the conventions as the one exception |

## What we did, step by step

### 1. Shared pieces
`types/customer.ts`, `types/salesOrder.ts`, `api/customers.ts`, `api/salesOrders.ts`, `getSellableProducts` (ACTIVE products with their price), `constants/orderStatus.ts` (labels and tones from the mockup, a copy of the state machine for the buttons, the actions and the steps), `utils/money.ts`, texts, routes. `PageHeader` got `titleExtra` (the status badge) and `subtitle`.

### 2. Pages
- **Customers:** list and form like Suppliers, with the Orders column; Delete only for managers.
- **Sales Orders:** search, customer and status filters, newest first.
- **New Sales Order:** a `Form.List` of lines, each with Available (from inventory-service, red when the line asks for more), Qty, Unit price and Line total; one line per product checked in the form; the estimated total; Place order goes to the new order's page.
- **Order detail:** badge, "created by", the buttons the status allows (Cancel with a confirmation, for managers), Ant Design `Steps` (a stopped order shows its step in red), the lines with the backend's totals, and a `Timeline` of the history.

## Problems we hit and how we fixed them

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| `getStockLevels` didn't accept `productId` | The frontend type had lost a filter the backend has; added to `StockLevelFilter` |
| Ant Design 6 renamed props (`Steps` items `content`, `orientation`; `Timeline` items `title` / `content`) | Read the `.d.ts` files first; no deprecation warnings in the console |
| My browser check counted timeline rows as steps | Ant Design 6 builds `Timeline` on `Steps`, so both use `.ant-steps-item-*`; the screenshots showed the pages were right |
| On phones the lines of a new order ran together | A divider between lines, on phones only |
| Pay, ship and complete can't be reached before the Phase 6 saga | For one test order, set RESERVED and its history row with SQL, as the saga will |

## New compared to Java 8/11 and Spring Boot 2.x

| Old | Now |
|---|---|
| `BigDecimal` for money everywhere | In the browser: whole cents as integers for an estimate (`toCents`, `lineTotalCents`); JavaScript has no `BigDecimal` |
| A list of rows in a form, managed by hand | Ant Design's `Form.List`: add, remove, and errors per line (`items[1].quantity` from the backend lands on that field) |

## Concepts explained during this task

- **Why integers for money:** `0.1 + 0.2` is `0.30000000000000004` in binary floating point; whole cents are exact.
- **Two copies of the state machine:** the frontend copy only hides buttons; the backend decides, and answers 409 if the copy is ever out of date.
